A bit about me

I'm studying B.Sc. CSIT at Tribhuvan University, and I learned most of what I know by building real things for real people: a shop's first website, a landing page for a campaign, a redesign for someone who was tired of their old one.

Along the way I picked up social media management and basic digital marketing, which turned out to matter more than I expected. A good site is only useful if people can find it and understand it in a few seconds.

I like clear communication, small steps, and finishing what I start. If you're not sure what you need yet, that's fine. We can work it out together.
